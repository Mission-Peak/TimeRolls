## Description (4000 max)

App Store descriptions are plain text — no bold, no markdown. Paste as-is.

Time Rolls is a quiet photo game. Each day brings a small challenge — a handful
of rounds, four photographs at a time, and one question worth answering.

Most of the photographs are ones worth recognising — a painting, a landmark, a
face from history, an animal, a plant. Every round also holds one photograph of
your own, taken from the pictures already on your device: a summer you remember,
a face you know, a place you have been. Three of ours to every one of yours,
which means a camera roll of thirty pictures lasts as long as a camera roll of
thirty thousand.

The questions are plain ones. Which of these is older? Which of these is a cat?
Which one shows Louis Pasteur? Where was this taken? When a question is hard
there is a hint, and when the hint is no help the answer is never far away.

Made to be easy to play

- Tap a card, or say "one", "two", "three" or "four" out loud.
- Swipe right, or say "next", to move on. Nothing is timed and nothing runs out.
- A wrong answer is met with a warm word and another try, never a buzzer.
- Turn a card over to read about the photograph, full screen, one at a time.
- Large type, high contrast, and a voice that reads the question aloud.
- Sound off means all of it off — the narration, the cues and the microphone.

For families and caregivers

Setup decides how long a day's challenge is, which themes appear, and which
photographs are used. A picture that shouldn't come up can be struck off and will
never be shown again. The Stars screen keeps a simple record: days played, sets
finished, best run, best week.

What we do with your photographs — nothing

Your photos never leave your device. They are drawn on the screen and, for the
Things game, looked at by your own device's photo recognition. No image is stored
by us and none is sent anywhere. There is no sign-in, no account, no advertising,
no analytics and no tracking of any kind. Nothing is sold, ever.

Tapping Share hands a picture to the iOS share sheet, where you choose who gets
it; it never passes through us. Our own photographs carry their photographer's
credit and licence with them.

Free

Time Rolls is free. There is nothing to buy inside it and there is no paid tier.

Time Rolls draws on activities inspired by cognitive stimulation, spaced-retrieval
memory practice, reminiscence, and coordination research used in dementia care.
It is a wellness and engagement tool; it is not a medical device; it does not
diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition; it does not slow, stop, or
reverse cognitive decline. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice.

## Review notes

No account or sign-in — tap through onboarding and play. On first launch the app
asks for photo library access; declining is fine, there is a "Play with the
built-in photos instead" path that uses the curated packs alone. Microphone and
speech access are optional and only used while a round waits for an answer;
recognition is on-device. Pack photographs are fetched from our storage provider
(OneBucket) over signed, short-lived URLs; the request carries nothing about the
user.
